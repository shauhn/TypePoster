Template & text

Question, Poll and Playlist use the Classic type layout. Your colors, fonts, grain and an optional photo behind the type still apply.

In your TODAY. archive, tap MAKE POSTER on a story, then tap this button. Or paste into the Headline box: the headline, caption and footer fill in by themselves.

Lines break automatically for the biggest type. Press return to break lines yourself.

Post the options first, then turn on results for a follow-up post.

One song per line, like “Song — Artist”. Dashes, “by”, tabs and | all work. Track numbers and times like 3:45 are tidied up, and the times add up to the total length. If the first line has no artist, it becomes the playlist name. Spotify “Copy link” gives only web addresses, so copy song names instead (Apple Music and most apps copy them as text).
